Styles and theming

Components are encapsulated in shadow roots, and styled with the platform's own tools: <style> in the export, custom properties that inherit through the shadow boundary, ::part(), and stylesheets adopted by the page. Toggle dark mode: every component on this page follows the page's colour tokens.

Component styles

<style> elements beside an export's <template> become the definition's styles. They are built into one constructed stylesheet per definition and adopted by every instance's shadow root, so a hundred cards share one sheet.

    Adopting into a shadow root
    
      
    An <html-import> adopts into the root that contains it. Inside a shadow root, the theme stays inside it. Likewise, a stylesheet a module adopts (like rating.html's gold) applies only inside that module's components.
