Form-associated components

form-associated on an export makes the component a form control: static formAssociated and ElementInternals. Its name and value reach FormData, it validates (:invalid), a disabled <fieldset> disables it, and it resets and restores with its form. form-control="input" says which control in the template carries the value; without it the element is the control.
